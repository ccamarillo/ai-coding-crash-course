# Glossary

Terms used across code, copy and docs. Use these words, not their synonyms.

- **Study day**: a calendar day, in the student's timezone, with at least one first lesson completion. Re-completing a lesson does not create a Study day. _Avoid:_ active day, activity.
- **Streak**: a run of consecutive Study days. The current Streak ends today or yesterday; otherwise it is 0. _Avoid:_ chain, progress streak.
